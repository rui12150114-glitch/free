About Freelance Rate Calculator

Freelance Rate Calculator is a free tool built for freelancers, consultants, and independent contractors who want to price their work based on real numbers instead of guesswork.

Most freelancers set their rates by picking a number that "feels right," copying what someone else charges, or simply matching their old salary divided by 2,080 hours. None of these approaches account for taxes, business expenses, unpaid administrative time, or vacation — all of which a freelancer has to cover on their own.

What this tool does

The calculator takes your desired take-home income, your working hours, your vacation time, an estimated tax rate, your business expenses, and your realistic billable utilization — then works out the hourly and daily rate you'd need to charge to hit your income goal.

Why we built it

Pricing is one of the hardest parts of freelancing, and small mistakes compound over a career. We wanted a straightforward, free calculator that shows the math clearly, without requiring an account, a subscription, or your email address.

What this tool is not

This calculator gives estimates for general planning purposes. It is not tax, legal, accounting, or financial advice, and it doesn't account for every detail of your specific situation — such as local tax brackets, deductions, or industry-specific pricing norms. For decisions that matter, talk to a qualified accountant or financial advisor.
