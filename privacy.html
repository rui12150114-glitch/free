Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Freelance Rate Calculator ("we," "our," or "the site") handles information when you use this website.

Calculator data

The numbers you enter into the calculator (income, hours, expenses, and similar figures) are processed entirely in your browser. They are not sent to, or stored on, any server. We have no way of seeing or accessing the values you type in.

Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics services to understand overall traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited and roughly how many people use the calculator. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

Third-party links

This site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party sites.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
